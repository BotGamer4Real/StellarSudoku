StellarSudoku Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026. This policy describes what StellarSudoku collects and why. It matches the app as shipped.

Who we are

StellarSudoku is published by BotGamer4Real. Contact: botgamer4real@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you play as a guest, progress stays on your device. We do not need an account for Single Player.

If you create an account we store:

  • Email address and authentication identifiers
  • Display name (shown on leaderboards)
  • Game progress: campaign unlocks, puzzle completions, coin balance, cosmetics owned, achievements, daily streak, and leaderboard times
  • In-progress boards so you can continue on another device

We do not collect precise location, advertising IDs for ads, or move-by-move telemetry for advertising. There are no ads and no real-money purchases in this version.

Why we collect it

Account data is used only to run the game: sign in, save progress, award coins, show leaderboards, and let you delete your account. We do not sell personal data.

Where it is stored

Account and cloud save data are stored in our Supabase project in the EU (Ireland). Guest data stays on the device and may be lost if you uninstall or clear app data.

Sharing

Display names and times appear on Daily and Campaign leaderboards. We use infrastructure providers (hosting and database) to operate the app. We do not share data with advertisers.

Children

Game content is suitable for a general audience. Accounts are not offered to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Account deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete account, then type DELETE. On the web (no app required): stellar-sudoku.vercel.app/delete-account.html. That removes your profile, progress, coins, achievements, and leaderboard rows. Signing in with the same email afterwards requires Sign up again.

Contact

Privacy questions: botgamer4real@gmail.com.
